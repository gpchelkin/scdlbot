🎉 scdlbot 1.0: rewritten from scratch Hi! I'm the bot that downloads music from SoundCloud, Bandcamp, YouTube and other sites. I've been rebuilt from the ground up, and here's what's new: ⚡ Faster, and no more freezing: when I'm busy, I tell you your place in the queue 🎬 YouTube as video: tap 🎵 Audio or 🎬 Video under a YouTube link, or send /video with a link 📸 Instagram: reels, posts with photos and videos (as albums), stories 🐦 Twitter / X: videos, GIFs, photos and text. TikTok and YouTube Shorts come as videos 💿 Albums and playlists come as Telegram albums, up to 50 tracks 🎧 Audio in its original quality (m4a or mp3) with tags and cover art, or always MP3 if you prefer (/settings) 📦 Files up to 1000 MB ⚙️ One change for you: YouTube links now ask whether you want 🎵 audio or 🎬 video, and everything else downloads right away as before. To get YouTube as audio right away again: /settings → Auto-download all. 👥 In groups, I ask before downloading; group admins can change that in /settings. Send me a link to try it out!
