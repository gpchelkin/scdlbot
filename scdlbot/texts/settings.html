Select the mode for messages without a command (simple messages with links): • Auto: download every supported link: tracks and YouTube videos as audio, Shorts, TikTok, Instagram and Twitter as videos. • Auto + ask about video: the same, but for YouTube videos ask whether you want 🎵 Audio or 🎬 Video (480p). Handy for group chats that share shorts and reels. • Ask: ask before downloading any link. Sane defaults are Auto + ask about video for private chats{private} and Ask for group chats{group}. Enable Captions to send audios with source info in captions and as replies to the original message. Enable Unknown sites to allow downloading from sites not in the list (see /help). Enable Always MP3 to get audio as MP3 instead of the original M4A, for players and devices that don't support M4A.
