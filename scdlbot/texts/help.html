Hi! I download and send audios of tracks/sets/albums/videos/playlists from free streaming websites in M4A or MP3 format with tags and artwork. Videos from TikTok, Instagram and X are sent as videos. Usage: Send or forward a message with links and I will: • In Download mode: download every link I support (or you may use a direct command /dl <links>, or reply /dl to a message with links) • In Ask mode: find supported links and ask you whether to download them • Use /settings to select the mode for messages without a command, toggle Captions and Unknown sites. • Privacy mode is disabled, so I see every group message, but I only react to links. • Albums and playlists are sent in groups of up to 10 tracks, up to {max_items} tracks per link.
