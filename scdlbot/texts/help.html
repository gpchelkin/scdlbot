Hi! I download and send audios of tracks/sets/albums/videos/playlists from free streaming websites in M4A or MP3 format with tags and artwork. Videos from TikTok, Instagram, Twitter and YouTube Shorts are sent as videos, their photos as photos, text-only tweets as text. Usage: Send or forward a message with links and I will: • In Download mode: download every link I support (or you may use a direct command /dl <links>, or reply /dl or @{bot} to a message with links, e.g. one I missed while I was offline) • In Ask mode: find supported links and ask you whether to download them • Use /settings to select the mode for messages without a command, toggle Captions and Unknown sites. • Privacy mode is disabled, so I see every group message, but I only react to links. • Albums and playlists are sent in groups of up to 10 tracks, up to {max_items} tracks per link.
